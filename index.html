Privacy Policy – Boomkin Ads API

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Boomkin uses the Amazon Ads API to access and manage advertising data for its own Amazon seller account.

Information We Access

Our application may access advertising campaign information, performance reports, keywords, budgets, impressions, clicks, and advertising sales data.

How We Use Information

We use this information to analyze advertising performance, generate business reports, and support advertising campaign management for our own business.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent advertising data to third parties. We do not share advertising data with unrelated third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect access credentials and advertising data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary for our business purposes and delete it when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable requirements.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the operator of the Boomkin application.
